# Your Age on Other Planets

Enter your birth date to calculate your age in years on every solar system body and count down to your next planetary birthday.

## Features
- Age calculated for Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune
- Countdown in Earth days to the next birthday on each body
- Fun fact about each planet displayed on its card
- Colorful card grid with planet emoji for each world

## Usage
Select your birthdate from the date picker. Cards for all eight planets plus the Moon will appear showing your age in that body's years, and how many Earth days until your next planetary birthday. A "Today!" label appears when a planetary birthday falls on the current date.
